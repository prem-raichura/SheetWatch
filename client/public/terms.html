02What SheetWatch does

SheetWatch is a web application that monitors Google Sheets for changes. After you connect your Google account and select a spreadsheet, a tab, and a cell range, SheetWatch fetches that range on a schedule you choose, compares it to the previous reading, and records any differences. It can then notify you through web push, email, Slack, Discord, a generic webhook, or Telegram, according to rules you configure.

The Service also provides supporting features:

  • Change history and per-sheet timelines
  • KPI tiles and charts built from cells inside a watched range
  • Alert rules that fire when values match conditions you define
  • Quiet hours, snoozing, pausing, and archiving
  • Scheduled PDF and CSV reports delivered by email
  • Cross-sheet comparison, which surfaces differences between a master sheet and target sheets and lets you apply them
  • Read-only public share boards at a secret URL

We may add, change, or remove features at any time. We will not remove a feature you actively depend on without reasonable notice in the app where practical.

06When SheetWatch writes to your sheets

Read the suggestion before you accept it. Writes are applied directly to live spreadsheets and SheetWatch cannot undo them. Use Google Sheets' own version history to recover from an unwanted change.

During routine monitoring, SheetWatch only reads. It writes in exactly two cases, both initiated by you:

  1. Accepting a comparison suggestion. When you accept a suggestion in the Compare feature, SheetWatch writes that single value into the identified cell of the target spreadsheet. If the target cell changed after the suggestion was raised, the suggestion is flagged as conflicted, and it is your responsibility to review it.
  2. Removing a spreadsheet. When you choose to remove a spreadsheet from the Sheets page, SheetWatch moves that file to your Google Drive trash, where Google's own retention rules apply and from which you can restore it.

You are solely responsible for reviewing any change before accepting it and for the consequences of changes applied to your spreadsheets through the Service.
