SheetWatch

Your Google Sheets, under watch.

SheetWatch is a web app that monitors Google Sheets for changes. Pick a spreadsheet, a tab, and a cell range. SheetWatch checks that range on the interval you choose, spots any cell that changed, and tells you — by web push, email, Slack, Discord, Telegram, or your own webhook.

What SheetWatch does

Google Sheets tells you a file changed. It does not tell you which cell, what the old value was, or ping the people who care. SheetWatch fills that gap.

Cell-level detection

Watch a specific range, or match rows by a key column. SheetWatch reports exactly which cell moved from what to what.

Alerts that route

Build rules from conditions like equals, greater than, or changes-to, and send each rule to the channels that should hear it.

KPI tiles and charts

Pin cells as live metrics with thresholds, and chart ranges as line, bar, area, or donut without leaving the app.

History you can scrub

Every detected change is kept with a before-and-after snapshot, so you can go back and see what the grid looked like.

Cross-sheet comparison

Compare a master sheet against target sheets, review the differences it finds, and apply the ones you accept.

Reports and share boards

Email yourself scheduled PDF or CSV summaries, and publish a read-only KPI board at a secret link.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Sign in with Google

    SheetWatch never sees your password. Google's own consent screen shows you exactly what you are granting, and you can decline or revoke it later.

  2. Pick a sheet and a range

    Choose a spreadsheet from your Drive or paste its link, then set the tab, the cell range, and how often it should be checked.

  3. Get told when it changes

    SheetWatch keeps checking in the background, even with your browser closed, and notifies you through whichever channels you turned on.

What SheetWatch does with your Google data

The short version: it reads the range you picked so it can tell when that range changes. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to train AI models.

Questions

The things people ask before signing in.

What is SheetWatch?

SheetWatch is a web application that monitors Google Sheets for changes. You connect your Google account, pick a spreadsheet, tab, and cell range, and SheetWatch checks that range on your chosen interval and notifies you the moment a cell changes.

Why does it need access to my Google account?

To detect a change, SheetWatch has to read the range you asked it to watch, and to let you pick a sheet from a list it has to see which spreadsheets you have. It only reads the range you selected, and it only writes when you explicitly accept a suggestion or remove a spreadsheet.

How quickly will I know about a change?

As fast as the poll interval you set for that sheet. SheetWatch checks on a schedule rather than receiving live events from Google, so a value that changes and changes back between two checks will not be spotted. Do not use it as the only safeguard for something critical.

Does it change my spreadsheets?

Not during monitoring — that is read-only. It writes in two cases you trigger yourself: accepting a cross-sheet suggestion writes that one value, and removing a spreadsheet moves the file to your Google Drive trash.

How much does it cost?

SheetWatch is free to use.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Account, then Danger zone, and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and SheetWatch's access to your Google account is revoked at the same time. Your spreadsheets are never touched — they stay in your Drive.
